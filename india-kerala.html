E-waste in India & Kerala

A fast-growing digital economy means fast-growing e-waste. Responsible systems and awareness are key.

India Kerala

India's e-waste challenge

Rapid tech adoption, short device lifecycles, and mixed formal/informal handling.

Scale

  • India is among the top e-waste generating countries globally.
  • Large volumes from households, offices, and IT parks.

Systems

  • Authorized recyclers and collection channels exist across states.
  • Producer responsibility and take-back schemes are expanding.

Opportunity

  • More awareness can shift devices from informal to formal channels.
  • Schools, colleges, and startups can lead collection drives.

Safe disposal in India

  • Use authorized collection points and certified recyclers.
  • Separate e-waste from household waste.
  • Wipe data and remove batteries before storage or handover.

Kerala context

High literacy and strong civic participation make Kerala well-placed to lead on e-waste awareness.

🏫

Schools and colleges

Campuses can run e-waste drives, repair cafes, and component libraries for robotics clubs.

🏙️

Urban local bodies

Municipalities can partner with authorized recyclers and run awareness campaigns in wards.

How e-waste is managed in Kerala

  • Collection through authorized dealers, producers, and recycling partners.
  • Sorting and safe dismantling at certified facilities.
  • Recovery of metals and safe disposal of hazardous parts.
  • Growing role of startups and student groups in awareness and collection.
