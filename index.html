Why it matters

Electronics are not ordinary waste.

They contain valuable materials such as copper and gold, but can also release hazardous chemicals when burned or dismantled unsafely.

Before a device leaves your hands

A quick checklist for responsible disposal.

01 · Keep or repair

Consider a repair, upgrade or donation before replacing it.

02 · Protect your data

Back up your files, sign out of accounts and erase personal information.

03 · Hand it over safely

Take it to an authorised recycler or manufacturer collection programme.
